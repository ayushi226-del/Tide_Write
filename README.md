## What is Tide Write?

When we're distressed, feelings often show up as a blur: "bad," "upset," "off." Tide Write guides a person from that blur to something more precise and more manageable, in a few calm steps. It never tries to "fix" or erase an emotion. The goal is to change your *relationship* to the feeling, not to make it disappear.

## The Psychology Behind It

The design rests on three well-established ideas:

1. **Affect labeling and emotional granularity.** Putting feelings into precise words helps organize experience, and people with a richer emotional vocabulary tend to regulate their emotions more effectively.
2. **Expressive writing (James Pennebaker).** Writing about emotional experiences helps people acknowledge their feelings and turn them into a coherent narrative, with documented wellbeing benefits.
3. **Mindfulness-based acceptance (urge surfing).** Observing a feeling with curiosity, like a wave that rises and falls, without acting on it or suppressing it.

> The research behind these ideas is well supported. How well any specific tool works depends on its design and on consistent use.

## How It Works

| Phase | What happens | Principle |
|---|---|---|
| **Arrive** | A guided breathing exercise and a 3-3-3 grounding exercise (see, feel, hear), each with space to write | Grounding before thinking |
| **Name It** | Enter one or two words for your feeling. If the word is vague, the app suggests more precise alternatives with short definitions | Emotional granularity |
| **Write** | Three optional prompts: what happened just before, where you feel it in your body, and what the feeling would say if it could speak | Expressive writing |
| **Surface** | A slow wave animation with gentle closing lines | Urge surfing |
| **Reflect and Carry** | See how your words shifted, choose one word to carry with you, then erase everything, keep it on your device, or save nothing | Closure and agency |

Every prompt can be skipped, and nothing is required.

## Design Principles

- **Calm and low-stimulation.** An ocean-inspired palette, slow motion, no notifications, no ads, no upsells.
- **Progressive disclosure.** One step on screen at a time, so it never feels overwhelming.
- **Acceptance-based language.** No "fixing," no "positive thinking," no scores.
- **Accessible.** Keyboard navigable, readable contrast, and animations disabled for users who prefer reduced motion.

## Privacy

- No accounts, no backend, no analytics, no tracking.
- Everything you write stays in your browser's memory during the session.
- Nothing is saved unless you choose **"Keep it on this device"** at the end, and that is stored only in your own browser (local storage).
- You can clear everything at any time.
